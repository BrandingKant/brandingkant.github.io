Santorini on a budget from India is the question, not the answer. The math is what makes it possible.

By Swami Chavan · Published 5 October 2026 · 9 min read

Santorini is sold as the most expensive sunset in Europe. The photos you've seen are real — blue domes, white cliff houses, the Aegean — but the price tag of that postcard is inflated by three industries: cruise ship day-trippers, luxury cave hotels, and the Oia sunset queue. The actual island, for Indians willing to plan carefully, is doable for ₹1.5-2 lakh from Delhi for 5 days — flights, visa, accommodation, food, ferries. Not cheap. But not the ₹4 lakh luxury package you've been quoted. Here's the math, the plan, and the three things that actually cost what they cost.

The visa: Schengen, through Greece

Santorini is in Greece, which is a Schengen country. You apply for a Schengen visa through the Greek embassy in India — via VFS Global. Greece has a moderate rejection rate for Indian first-timers, lower than France. The visa is €90 (₹8,100) plus VFS service fee ₹1,500-2,500. The process takes 10-15 days.

Important: your itinerary must genuinely include Greece as the main destination. If your Schengen visa was issued by France and you land in Athens, you may face questions. If your visa is from Greece, you enter through Greece. Plan accordingly.

The flights: the single biggest variable

Delhi to Santorini has no direct flights. The route is Delhi → Athens (15-20 hours with a layover) → Santorini by ferry or flight. Options: 1. Delhi-Istanbul-Athens on Turkish Airlines: ₹45,000-55,000 return. Istanbul layover of 3-7 hours each way — doable, not exhausting. 2. Delhi-Dubai-Athens on Emirates: ₹50,000-60,000 return. Better connection quality. 3. Delhi-Mumbai-Athens on IndiGo + connecting: ₹35,000-45,000 return if booked early.

Athens to Santorini: two options. Flight on Aegean / Olympic Air — €80-150 (₹7,500-14,000) one way, 50 minutes. Or, the slow option: ferry from Piraeus (Athens port) — Blue Star Ferries, €35-55 (₹3,200-5,000) one way in economy deck, 8 hours. The ferry is the experience — the Aegean from the deck, the islands you pass. It's also how to save ₹7,000+ one way.

Contrarian call: fly to Santorini (fast), return by ferry (slow, experience, saves money). Best of both.

Day 1: Athens — the city before the island

Land in Athens. Stay one night — Central Athens hotels €50-80 (₹4,500-7,000/night). Day 1: the Acropolis (€30 entry from April-October, €20 winter; ~₹2,700, book skip-the-line online: etickets.tap.gr). The Acropolis Museum (€15, ~₹1,300). Walk Plaka (the old neighborhood). Greek taverna dinner: souvlaki plate €8-12 (~₹800-1,080), house wine €5 (~₹450) per glass. Late-night flight or next-morning ferry to Santorini.

Day 2-4: Santorini — the island minus the Oia trap

Stay in Fira (the capital) or Kamari (the black beach). Not Oia. Oia is where the luxury cave hotels are (€200-800/night). Fira has pension-style hotels €50-80/night (₹4,500-7,000) — walk to the caldera in 5 minutes, same view, 30% of the Oia price. Kamari (a village on the east coast, black sand beach) has €40-60/night rooms and is the quiet option.

Day 2: Fira walk down to the old port (cable car €6 or donkey path free). Caldera walk — take the path north from Fira toward Firostefani and Imerovigli. The path is 3 km along the cliff, caldera on one side, village houses on the other. This is what the postcards show — and it's free. Do it at 10 AM or 4 PM, not mid-day.

Day 3: Oia — go at 10 AM, not sunset. The village is quiet at 10 AM. The blue domes, the narrow lanes, the castle ruins — all yours. By 4 PM, 10 cruise-ship groups arrive and it's a scrum. By sunset, Oia is the most crowded viewpoint in the country and your sunset photo will have a stranger's face in it. Morning Oia is the only Oia worth doing.

Day 4: Akrotiri archaeological site (€12, ~₹1,080) — the "Greek Pompeii" buried by volcanic ash around 1600 BC. 30 minutes from Fira by bus (€1.80, ~₹160). Then Red Beach (free) — a short walk from Akrotiri, black and red cliffs, narrow stretch of sand. Or Perissa beach — the largest and best for swimming.

The sunset: where to actually watch it

Skip Oia sunset. Two options that are actually better: 1. Imerovigli caldera path — the highest point of the caldera cliff, 90-minute walk from Fira. Sunset view over the volcano, Skaros rock, and the Aegean — but no crowd. 2. From a boat. Sunset caldera cruises depart from the old port in Fira at 5:30 PM, 4-5 hours, €50-70 per person (~₹4,500-6,200) including dinner and wine. The boat anchors off Oia and you watch the sunset from the water — it's what the cliff crowds are straining to see.

What it actually costs (October 2026)

5 days, two people, mid-budget: 1. Schengen visa (per person, one-time): ₹10,000 2. Delhi-Athens return flights (per person): ₹48,000 3. Athens-Santorini flight one-way (per person): ₹10,000 4. Santorini-Athens ferry one-way (per person): ₹4,500 5. Hotels: 1 night Athens (€60 / ₹5,500) + 3 nights Santorini (€60/night / ₹5,500/night x 3) = ₹22,000 total for two, ₹11,000 PP 6. Food: €30/day PP = ₹2,700/day x 5 days = ₹13,500 PP at taverna breakfast, souvlaki lunch, taverna dinner 7. Acropolis + Akrotiri + museum entries = ₹5,000 PP 8. Caldera cruise sunset = ₹5,500 PP 9. Local transport (bus, taxi, ferry return) = ₹3,000 PP

Total per person: ₹115,500 (including the one-time visa fee). For two: ₹231,000. The biggest chunk is flights — ₹58,500 of the ₹115,500. If you book flights 3-4 months out and take the ferry both directions (adding one day), cost drops further.

The ₹4 lakh package quote: includes the luxury cave hotel in Oia (€250-400/night), private transfers, sunset dinner at a restaurant in Oia, and the convenience premium. The same sunsets from Fira cost ₹1.15 lakh less per person.

Three things to know about Santorini costs

1. The season matters. October is shoulder — hotels 30-40% cheaper than July-August, weather still 23-26C, water swimmable. November is end of season — many hotels close. The island has a 5-month year. Visit between May and October. 2. Tavernas, not restaurants. Tavernas are family-run Greek restaurants. Moussaka, souvlaki, Greek salad, fresh fish by weight. Budget €20-25/2-person meal. "Restaurants" on the caldera edge are €60-100 for two. Same food, different view premium. 3. Cash. Greek islands still run heavily on cash. Bring €200-300 in cash from India (RBI allows $3,000 per trip). ATMs exist in Fira but run out in peak season.

FAQ

Do I need a Schengen visa to go to Santorini? Yes — Greece is in the Schengen area. Apply through the Greek embassy via VFS in India. Same Schengen visa process as France, Italy, or any other Schengen country.

Is Santorini safe for solo Indian women travellers? Yes — violent crime is rare. The bigger risk is dehydration on the caldera walks in summer — carry water, start early.

Can I do Santorini as a day trip from Athens? Technically — day flights are €120-180 return. But you'll see 4 hours of the island. Not worth it. Minimum 2 nights.

What about Mykonos instead? Mykonos is the party island — more clubs, higher prices. Santorini is the scenic island. For most Indian travellers, Santorini is the first Greek island worth visiting. Mykonos for the second trip.
