How to Spot AI-Generated Content and Deepfakes: A Practical Guide for India

By Swami Chavan·Published 2026-11-04·8 min read

The old rule — count the fingers, look at the ears — is obsolete. Current image and video models get hands right. What remains reliable is not visual inspection at all: it is verification of source, and a few language habits that models still cannot fully shake.

Text: the tells that survived

  • Perfectly balanced paragraphs with no specific detail — real expertise leaks specifics: prices, dates, names, failures.
  • Confident claims with no source, and no acknowledgement of uncertainty.
  • Symmetrical structure: every section the same length, every list exactly five items.
  • Vocabulary that avoids contractions and never risks a joke.
  • Statistics that are plausible but untraceable — search the number, it will not appear anywhere reputable.

Voice: the scam that is costing people money

Voice cloning needs a few seconds of audio from a social media video. The 'family emergency' call in a cloned relative's voice is now a real pattern in India. The defence is a family code word agreed in advance — not a question the caller can answer from your public posts, such as your mother's name.

Video: verify the source, not the pixels

A convincing video of a public figure is now cheap to make. Three checks beat any pixel analysis: does the clip exist on the person's official channel; does a second independent outlet report the same event; and does the timeline make sense with known dates. If only one account has it and it is asking you to act urgently, treat it as false.

Images: the metadata and reverse search check

  • Reverse image search the frame — AI images often have no earlier history anywhere.
  • Check the metadata: many generated images carry software tags, though social platforms strip them.
  • Look for text inside the image — signage and labels remain a weak point, especially in Indian scripts.
  • Check reflections and shadows for physical consistency across the whole frame.

The habit that protects you most

Urgency is the signal. Every deepfake scam, fake job offer, fake investment group and cloned-voice call needs you to act before you check. Slowing down by ten minutes and calling the person on a number you already have defeats nearly all of it.

The verdict

Do not try to out-analyse the pixels. Verify the source, demand a second independent report, and use a family code word for money emergencies. Urgency is the tell that never fails.

Quick answers

Can AI detection tools be trusted?

Not as proof. Text detectors produce false positives on human writing and miss edited AI text routinely. Use them as one weak signal, never as evidence.

What should I do if I receive a deepfake voice call asking for money?

Hang up, call the person back on a number you already have, and never transfer money on the basis of a voice alone. Agree a family code word in advance.

Are deepfake videos illegal in India?

Creating or sharing deceptive synthetic media can attract legal action under Indian law, including provisions on impersonation and cheating. Report such content to the platform and, for financial fraud, to the cybercrime portal.
