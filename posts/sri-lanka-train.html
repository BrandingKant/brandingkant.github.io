Sri Lanka by train is the best ₹30,000 trip in the world. Here's the route.

By Swami Chavan · Published 4 October 2026 · 8 min read

Every list of the world's great train journeys puts Switzerland and Japan on top and forgets the one that costs less than both combined: the Colombo–Kandy–Ella line. Tea-estate hills, waterfalls you can touch from the window, second-class tickets that cost 400 rupees. I've done it twice. Sri Lanka is what Goa was 30 years ago, with better food. Here's the full route.

Getting in: ETA in 10 minutes

Indians apply for the Sri Lanka ETA online — official portal — roughly USD 25–50 depending on processing, approved same-day usually, 30 days. Cheap flights from Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai: ₹12,000–18,000 return. Do the math: the whole trip costs less than a weekend in Goa during season.

The route, day by day

Day 1–2: Colombo — skip it, honestly. Take the airport taxi straight to Kandy (3 hours, 12,000 LKR). Kandy Lake, the Temple of the Tooth, and the best devilled pork in the hills. Day 3: The Kandy–Ella train. Book second-class observation car seats on the official railway site the day tickets open. The 7-hour ride is the trip's centrepiece — Nanu Oya's tea carpets, the Nine Arch Bridge at Ella approach, waterfalls at arm's length. Day 4–5: Ella — Nine Arch Bridge (walk it at sunrise), Little Adam's Peak (free, 45 minutes up), Ravana Falls. Ella's guesthouses: ₹2,500–4,000/night with views that would cost ₹40,000 in Munnar. Day 6: Down to Mirissa for the beaches — stilt fishermen sunsets, whale watching in season (December–April, 8,000–12,000 LKR). Day 7: Galle Fort, then the expressway bus back to Colombo airport.

What things cost (October 2026)

Rice and curry (the real one, unlimited): 500–900 LKR (₹125–225). Three-wheeler hops: 300–500 LKR. Tuktuk apps (PickMe) work in cities and end the meter negotiation entirely. Guesthouses: ₹2,500–4,000. The entire week on the ground: ₹15,000–20,000. With flights: under ₹35,000. It is, rupee for rupee, the best-value international trip from India right now.

Three things to know

1. Carry USD for the ETA and small notes — Indian cards work at ATMs everywhere but the arrival counters prefer dollars. 2. Sri Lanka has two monsoons — the southwest (Yala, May–September) and the northeast (Maha, October–January). December–April is the sweet spot for the hill country and south coast together. 3. Arrack. The local coconut spirit is genuinely world-class and the only thing on this trip you can't find a substitute for in India.

FAQ

Is Sri Lanka safe for Indian tourists in 2026? Yes — the 2022 economic crisis settled, tourism is fully back, and the tourist zones are as safe as they've ever been. Ordinary Sri Lankans are the friendliest neighbours you'll travel among.

Can I use Indian rupees? No — convert to LKR. USD converts better everywhere.

Train seats sold out? Second and third class are unreserved — you stand for the first hour, then seats open up. The ride is worth standing for.

How many days are enough? Seven covers Kandy–Ella–south coast well. Ten if you add Sigiriya and the cultural triangle.
