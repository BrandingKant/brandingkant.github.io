Buying a Refurbished Phone in India: The Checklist That Saves You

By Swami Chavan·Published 4 November 2026·8 min read

Why the savings are real, and the risk is too

A two-year-old flagship at 40 percent off new is genuinely good hardware. The risk is not the discount, it is the hidden condition: a replaced screen, a swollen battery, or a phone reported stolen and blocked by the operator. Each of those is checkable in minutes if you know what to look for.

The seven checks, in order

  • IMEI match: dial *#06# and compare with the box, the settings page and the invoice. All three must match.
  • Battery health: on iPhone check Settings - Battery - Battery Health. On Android use a diagnostic app. Below 85 percent, budget for a replacement.
  • Screen originality: check for uneven brightness, dead pixels on a white background, and touch response at all four corners.
  • Water damage indicators: ask the seller to show the SIM tray area. Any corrosion is an immediate no.
  • Camera: shoot a photo in bright light and inspect for dust spots, focus hunting and stabilisation rattles.
  • Charging: test the port with a cable you know works, and check wireless charging if the model supports it.
  • Account lock: on iPhone confirm Activation Lock is off. On Android confirm no Google account is signed in and FRP is clear.

The two red flags that end it

First, a seller who refuses an IMEI check or a video of the device powering on. Second, a price far below the market for that model - it usually means a blocked IMEI, a repaired board, or a stolen unit. There is no such thing as a genuine 60 percent discount on a current model.

Warranty and paperwork

Buy from platforms that offer a written warranty of at least six months and a return window of seven days. Keep the invoice with the IMEI printed on it - without it, warranty claims and resale both become difficult. For iPhones, check the service coverage status using the serial number on the manufacturer's website before paying.

The verdict

Buy refurbished only with a written warranty, a return window, and a verified IMEI. The three checks worth doing first: battery health, screen originality, account lock.

Quick answers

Is buying a refurbished iPhone in India safe?

It can be, if Activation Lock is off, battery health is above 85 percent, and the seller provides a written warranty. Avoid units without an invoice.

How much should a refurbished phone cost?

Roughly 55 to 70 percent of the new price depending on age and condition. Anything below 50 percent of the current market price for a recent model is a warning sign, not a bargain.

Does a refurbished phone come with warranty?

Reputable sellers provide six to twelve months of seller warranty. Manufacturer warranty usually applies only if the device was never activated and is under a year old.
