UK Standard Visitor visa for Indian travellers — the honest playbook with real costs and refusal-proofing

By Swami Chavan · Published 3 November 2026 · 10 min read

The UK visitor visa has a reputation among Indian travellers that it half deserves. Yes, refusals happen — but they happen for legible reasons, almost always about money's story rather than the money itself. The Standard Visitor visa costs £115 for six months, the process runs through VFS with biometrics, and the decision guidance is public. I've read enough of those refusal letters to tell you the four sentences that sink files, and the good news is none of them are hard to fix. Here's the honest playbook. Figures as observed late 2026.

The honest pitch

What you're applying for: a Standard Visitor visa, £115 for the 6-month version, allowing stays up to 6 months per visit for tourism, family visits and short business. The 2-year (£432), 5-year (£771) and 10-year (£963) versions exist and cost the same effort — worth it only once your travel pattern justifies them; first-time applicants are usually issued the 6-month. Processing guidance is about 3 weeks, and it's honest: files are read by humans, so build for the reader.

What the file really needs

DocumentThe honest standard
Online application + feeGov.uk form first, fee ₹12,500-13,000 at today's rates (£115), then VFS appointment
BiometricsFingerprints + photo at the VFS centre; pick the free standard slot, skip the paid "premium" lounges
Financial evidence6 months' bank statements, stamped, with credits matching your stated income — not a lump-sum top-up
Income proofSalary slips 3-6 months / ITRs 2-3 years / business GST + registration for self-employed
Employment letterStating role, tenure, salary and sanctioned leave with return date
Itinerary + stayBookings matching the stated plan; every day accounted for, including whom you're visiting if family
Sponsor documents (if hosted)Invitation letter, their status proof, their bank statement — the host funds the stay, say it plainly
Cover letterOne page, three jobs: who I am, why the trip makes sense, why I'm coming back

The four refusal sentences, paraphrased: your funds look deposited, not earned; your stated purpose doesn't match your bookings; your circumstances show weak ties to return to; your host's documents don't hold. Every one of those is a story you can fix before filing — and none of them is about being poor. A ₹6-lakh salary with clean statements beats a ₹30-lakh balance that landed last Tuesday.

The timeline that actually works

Week 1: Gov.uk application + VFS appointment (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai and others run daily slots in season). Week 2: Biometrics, document upload, passport submission. Weeks 3-5: The honest wait — most decisions land inside 15 working days; priority services at extra cost buy speed, not favour. Book refundable travel and don't plan a departure inside 6 weeks of filing. Decisions arrive by courier or passport pickup; keep the decision letter either way.

What nobody tells you

Keep every refusal letter you ever receive — a prior refusal is not a lifetime ban, and a well-explained second application with changed circumstances routinely succeeds. Contrarian call: do not use " visa consultants" who offer bank-statement top-ups; that's not paperwork help, that's fraud, and the reader of your file has seen every trick. And the 2-year multiple-entry visa is the best value in UK travel once you've had one clean visit — same effort, four trips' worth of door.

Read the UK's visa policy on Wikipedia — the Standard Visitor framework is published in caseworker guidance, which is the most honest visa documentation in the anglophone world. Read it once and the process stops being mystery and becomes process.

The Traveltico dossier: this place, properly

Location history & important places

The Standard Visitor visa consolidated the UK's older visit categories in 2019 into one route; for Indians it runs through the same VFS network as biometric enrolment worldwide. The fee history matters: the six-month route held at £95 for years and moved to £115 in April 2025, with the multi-year tiers repricing alongside. Important places: the gov.uk application form, VFS centres across India, and the decision letters issued under Appendix V of the Immigration Rules.

The local myth

The myth of the 'visa interview' — the UK visitor route has no interview; your file speaks entirely through documents, which is exactly why the cover letter and the bank statement's story decide outcomes before any human meets you.

Special points

UK decisions come with a stated, appealable rationale — refusal letters cite the exact paragraph of the rules — making it the most legible major visa process for Indian applicants: you can read the rules you were measured against, and reapply against them.

Quick answers

How much is a UK visitor visa for Indians?

£115 for the six-month Standard Visitor visa — roughly ₹12,500-13,000 plus VFS service charges. The 2-year (£432), 5-year (£771) and 10-year (£963) versions cost the same effort; first-time applicants are usually issued six months.

How long does a UK visa take from India?

The published standard is about 3 weeks from biometrics, and it's honest — most decisions land inside 15 working days. Priority services buy speed, not favour. Don't book non-refundable travel inside 6 weeks of filing.

Why do UK visas get refused for Indian applicants?

For legible reasons: bank statements that show sudden deposits, a stated purpose that contradicts the bookings, weak evidence of ties to India, or host documents that don't hold. Every refusal letter states the reason, and a corrected file routinely succeeds next time.
