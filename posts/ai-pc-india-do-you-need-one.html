AI PCs in India: Do You Actually Need One in 2026?

By Swami Chavan·Published 2026-11-04·7 min read

'AI PC' has become this year's sticker. Underneath the label is a specific thing: a processor with a neural processing unit capable of running small models locally. That is a real capability. Whether it changes your working day is a different question.

What the NPU in a laptop actually runs

Local transcription, live captions, background blur in video calls, photo editing models and small text assistants. These run with lower battery cost than doing the same work on the CPU. For meeting-heavy work, live captions and local transcription are the genuinely useful parts.

Where it makes no difference at all

  • Browsing, spreadsheets, email and documents — identical performance with or without an NPU.
  • Gaming — the GPU decides this, not the NPU.
  • AI chatbots in the browser — those run on servers regardless of your laptop.
  • Video editing exports — GPU and RAM matter far more.

The buying decision, in order

RAM first: 16GB is the real minimum for AI-assisted workflows, because local models need memory. Storage second: models and assets consume space. Then the display, then the battery. The NPU is a tiebreaker, not a headline.

The honest advice

If you were buying a laptop this year anyway, a current-generation chip with an NPU costs you nothing extra and may help later. If you are upgrading only for the AI label, do not. The features that justify it are still maturing, and the same money buys a better screen or more RAM — both of which you will notice daily.

The verdict

An AI PC is worth buying, but not worth upgrading for. Prioritise RAM, display and battery; treat the NPU as a free bonus that comes with the current chip generation.

Quick answers

Is an AI PC faster than a normal laptop?

Not for ordinary tasks. The NPU accelerates specific AI workloads such as transcription and image models; browsing, office work and gaming perform the same.

How much RAM does an AI PC need?

16GB is the practical minimum for local AI features and general multitasking. 32GB helps if you run local models or edit video.

Will AI features stop working on older laptops?

Cloud-based AI features keep working. Local features that require an NPU are limited to newer chips, so older laptops miss those specific extras.
