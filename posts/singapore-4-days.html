Singapore in 4 days for under ₹60,000: the itinerary that skips nothing important

By Swami Chavan · Published 4 October 2026 · 8 min read

Singapore is the one destination every Indian family puts on the list and then panics about the cost. Here's the truth from my three trips: Singapore is expensive the way a buffet is expensive — you can pay for everything, or you can eat strategically. Four days, all the iconic stuff, ₹55,000–60,000 per person including flights. Here's the exact build.

Getting in: the visa non-drama

Indians apply through an authorised agent (₹2,300–3,000, 3–5 working days) — Singapore doesn't do direct individual applications anymore. Better news: if you hold a valid US, UK, Canada, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, or Switzerland visa or residence, you qualify for a 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility — VFTF — when transiting. Check your eligibility at the official MFA portal before paying anyone.

Day 1: Gardens by the Bay + Marina Bay loop

Do this first, not last — it anchors everything. Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens are free; Cloud Forest + Flower Dome combo is 32 SGD (₹2,100). The Supertree light show (Garden Rhapsody, 7:45 PM and 8:45 PM, free) is the single best free experience in Singapore. Walk the Marina Bay loop after — Merlion, Esplanade, the skyline. One day, zero regrets.

Day 2: Sentosa, minus the tourist tax

Skip the cable car (35 SGD). Take the Sentosa Express from VivoCity — 4 SGD, and your Sentosa attractions can be bought à la carte. The Luge is worth it (2 rides ≈ 25 SGD). The beaches (Siloso, Palawan) are free. Universal Studios is 83 SGD — do it only if it's a family trip with kids under 12; otherwise it's a hot queue with Orlando-grade wait times.

Day 3: Chinatown + Little India + Hawker economics

This is the day Singapore becomes affordable. Maxwell Food Centre: Hainanese chicken rice at 5 SGD. Chinatown Complex: 3–4 SGD plates. Tekka Centre in Little India: butter chicken + naan better than most Delhi weddings, under 8 SGD. Buddha Tooth Relic Temple: free. Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple: free. The ethnic quarters are the real Singapore — the malls are the same Zara you have in Phoenix Mills.

Day 4: Zoo or nothing

The Singapore Zoo + River Wonders combo (or the Night Safari) is world-class and worth its 40–50 SGD. Then Changi's Jewel — the Rain Vortex — on the way out, because it's inside the airport and free. The perfect last day.

Transport and staying smart

Stay in Geylang or Kallang (₹6,000–7,500/night for clean doubles) — 10 minutes from downtown by MRT, half the price of Marina Bay hotels. Get an EZ-Link card (10 SGD with 5 SGD stored value) — every MRT ride is 1–2 SGD. Singapore's public transport makes taxis unnecessary. A 4-day transport spend: under 20 SGD.

Real 4-day budget (October 2026)

Flights ₹20,000–26,000 return (IndiGo/Air India from Chennai, Mumbai, or Delhi) · visa ₹2,700 · stay ₹13,500 (3 nights) · attractions ₹9,000 · food ₹4,500 (hawker centres, one fancy meal) · transport ₹1,300 · total: ₹51,000–57,000. The Instagram version with Marina Bay Sands rooms is triple.

FAQ

Is Singapore worth it for Indian families? Yes — it's the cleanest, safest, most walkable introduction to international travel. Kids under 4 travel free on trains and get free entry to most attractions.

Best time to visit? February–April. It's equatorial — always 30°C, always a chance of a 4 PM shower. Pack a 200-rupee umbrella from Mustafa, not a 2,000-rupee one from Decathlon.

How many days are enough? Four is the sweet spot. Beyond that you're paying Singapore prices to be bored.

Can I drink the tap water? Yes — Singapore tap water is potable. Refill your bottle and save 2 SGD a day.
