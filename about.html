About Travelingo

By Swami Chavan · Updated 2026-10-04

Travelingo exists because most travel content for Indians is written in one of two voices: the sponsored "hidden gem" that turns out to have a cover charge, or the listicle that has clearly never bought the ticket it describes.

Every guide here is written by me, Swami Chavan — a traveller and digital media professional with 12+ years of experience. I write the trips I've actually taken, in rupees, with the mistakes included. When I say a plate of bun cha costs 50,000 dong, that's the price I paid. When I say skip the Burj Khalifa "At the Top", that's because I paid for it, twice, and regretted it, twice.

What you'll find here

1. Destination guides with day-by-day routes that respect your leave balance. 2. Real budgets — the number you'll actually spend, not the number that makes a good headline. 3. Visa explainers written in plain language, with the official links so you never pay an agent for a 5-minute form. 4. The contrarian calls — which famous thing is genuinely worth it, and which one is marketing.

What you won't find

Sponsored "reviews", invented prices, AI-fluffed listicles, or anything that asks you to buy before it earns your trust. Affiliate links may appear in the future — they'll be disclosed, and they'll never change a single word of what gets written.
